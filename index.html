RootDonate

Online AI with live web search
Online AI is ready when you are.
Conversation history stays in this browser. Messages are sent to RootDonate’s Firebase AI function, which uses an online AI provider and searches Wikipedia for relevant web results. Voice input is optional; its transcript is only sent after you press Send.

Privacy & GDPR Notice

Conversation history is stored in this browser’s local storage. When you send a message, it is transmitted to the RootDonate Firebase callable function in the European West 2 region. The function searches Wikipedia and forwards the prompt and search snippets to its configured online AI provider. Do not send sensitive information. Optional voice recognition is provided by your browser; recognized text is placed in the message field and is not sent until you choose Send.
