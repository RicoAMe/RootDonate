Connecting to AI…
Chat history is stored in this browser. Messages are sent to the RootDonate Firebase function and the configured AI provider. Optional voice input uses your browser’s speech recognition and may be processed by its vendor. Voice starts only when you press the microphone button.

Privacy & GDPR Notice

Conversation memory and history are kept in this browser’s local storage. Messages you send are transmitted to a Firebase callable function in the configured Firebase project, then to the AI provider to generate a reply. Do not send sensitive information. Microphone access is optional and user-initiated; browser speech recognition may process audio through your browser vendor. Clearing site data removes local chat history.
